# Licensing status

This local repository is not assigned a blanket open-source license. Publication and a license choice for project-authored material are still pending. The presence of visible source is not itself permission to reuse it under MIT, GPL or another license.

Microsoft Zune software, original/derivative UI resources and third-party artwork are not relicensed by this project. Historical binary deltas and compressed helper components have mixed provenance. Do not interpret their packaging as a determination of redistribution rights.

NAudio and the bundled .NET runtime have their own notices, retained in `ZuneCrossfade-Licenses.txt`. Their licenses do not license Microsoft Zune or the rest of this repository. See `LICENSING-AND-SOURCES.md` for the preserved engineering notes.

Before publication, decide and document an appropriate scope for licensing the project's own code, and review what payload/artwork can be distributed. This stage deliberately makes no false claim that the entire modified Zune application is open source.
